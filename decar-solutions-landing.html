FAQ

Questions we hear before "let's talk."

How long does a typical project take?+
Most MVPs ship in 6–10 weeks; larger platforms and multi-service builds run longer. You'll get a real timeline after discovery, not a guess.
Do you work with fixed price or hourly billing?+
Both — fixed-scope projects get a fixed quote; ongoing or evolving work runs on a retainer. We'll recommend whichever fits your project.
Can you build on top of tools we already use?+
Yes — most automation work connects to your existing CRM, e-commerce platform, or ops tools rather than replacing them.
What happens after launch?+
Every project includes a support window post-launch, and ongoing maintenance/growth retainers are available after that.
